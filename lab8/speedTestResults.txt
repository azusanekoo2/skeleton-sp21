For random insertion, MyHashMap is much faster than ULLMap because it uses hashing and separate chaining, so insertions are amortized constant time when keys are well distributed. ULLMap must scan a list, so it grows much more slowly.

For in-order insertion, MyHashMap still performs well because hash maps do not depend on key order. This differs from an unbalanced BSTMap, which can degrade badly on sorted input.

For different bucket types, the results mostly show constant-factor differences. Since MyHashMap searches each bucket by iterating over the Collection, TreeSet and HashSet buckets do not automatically provide logarithmic or constant-time bucket lookup in this implementation.
